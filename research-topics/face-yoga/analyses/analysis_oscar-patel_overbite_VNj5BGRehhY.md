# Summary: How I Naturally Fixed My Overbite

- **Video**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VNj5BGRehhY
- **Channel**: Oscar Patel
- **Topic**: Natural correction of overbite/deep bite through palate expansion, chewing, and posture

## Key Points
- Claims to have improved deep bite within 9 months (ages 19-20), not over many years
- Root cause chain: anterior pelvic tilt -> superficial front line stretch -> forward head posture -> digastric/SCM muscles pull lower jaw back -> overbite
- Ancestors had 50mm palates vs modern average 34mm; narrow palate forces teeth into misalignment
- Criticizes orthodontics that push teeth posteriorly, arguing this narrows the arch and obstructs the airway
- References John Mew's case study showing overbite correction through upper jaw expansion alone
- Three-step approach: fix body posture, expand palate, fix chewing mechanics

## Method/Routine
1. **Body posture**: RSS method (release fascia, stretch hip flexors, strengthen glutes), train back of neck, fix walking pattern (toes in, spiral movement)
2. **Palate expansion**:
   - Thumb pulling 1-1.5 min every hour (minimum 6x/day)
   - Incisive papilla pushing with thumbs while chin tucking
   - Tongue chewing: push mastic gum against T-spot repeatedly throughout day
3. **Bilateral forward chewing**:
   - Split mastic gum in two, one each side
   - Bite down and roll jaw forward (counterclockwise rotation)
   - Also rip food with front teeth to get mind-muscle connection with masseters
4. **Sleep protocol**: Zygotape, sleep on side, pillow between legs; never sleep on back with overbite
- Warning: Do NOT walk around with lower jaw pushed forward artificially — teeth must touch while tongue is on roof of mouth
