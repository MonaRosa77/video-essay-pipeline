# Summary: How to Fix Your Side Profile

- **Video**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IjZhXNMKE4g
- **Channel**: Oscar Patel
- **Topic**: Fixing a recessed side profile through myofunctional habits and exercises

## Key Points
- A recessed jaw is not natural; it results from poor habits in chewing, swallowing, breathing, and posture (the "four key myofunctional habits")
- Chewing induces mechanotransduction, which creates osteoblasts and develops bone structure; each bite should be chewed 25+ times
- Tongue chewing (pressing gum against palate with tongue tip) activates the genioglossus muscle and releases fascial tension, enabling proper tongue posture
- Back neck muscles (not just front) are critical for cranium support; neck extensions with weighted plates are recommended
- Functional training (sprinting, mace bell, Indian club bell) supports overall postural health better than isolated muscle training
- Zygomatic tape and mentalis tape can deactivate compensatory muscles, encouraging proper lip seal, tongue posture, and nasal breathing during sleep
- Nasion tapping with a gua sha can superficially straighten a nose bump; zygo pulling can develop cheekbones

## Method/Routine
1. Fix four key habits: chewing, swallowing, breathing, posture
2. Chew each bite 25+ times with proper foods (meat, hard foods)
3. Practice tongue chewing with natural gum for 15 minutes daily
4. Train back neck muscles with weighted neck extensions
5. Do functional training (sprints, mace bell)
6. Optional: nasion growth technique with gua sha, zygo pulling for cheekbones
7. Wear zygomatic tape and mentalis tape (especially while sleeping) to retrain muscles passively
