# Summary: Face Workout Routine — Train Your Facial Muscles

- **Video**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w-wOER0xRZI
- **Channel**: Oscar Patel
- **Topic**: Specific exercises to train facial muscles for improved appearance and function

## Key Points
- The face is more adaptable than the body due to sutures and epicranial fascia
- Training facial muscles changes appearance: masseters (wider face), orbicularis oris (hollow cheeks), orbicularis oculi (better eyes), zygomaticus major (less bloated)
- Deactivating negative muscles (mentalis, buccinator) is equally important as activating positive ones
- Warns against chewing hard gum with asymmetrical palates — can cause TMJ and asymmetry
- Tongue chewing (pushing gum against palate with tongue tip) activates masseters without creating asymmetry
- Neck training is included as foundational for head/face support

## Method/Routine
1. **Masseters** (3 exercises):
   - Frown and bite down (2 min while eating hard food) for mind-muscle connection
   - AeroPit breathing device on max settings (5 min on bike/treadmill after gym, 2-3x/week)
   - Tongue chewing with mastic gum (push against roof of mouth with tongue tip)
2. **Orbicularis oris**:
   - CPS exercise second half: suck cheeks in, create loudest popping sound (10 reps, 2 sets)
   - Any position holding a scrunched/kissing face
3. **Orbicularis oculi** (eyes):
   - Pull outer corner of eye, squeeze (20 reps, 2 sets, before sleep)
   - Massage/deactivate frontalis muscle (30 sec pinching) to reduce upper eyelid exposure
4. **Mentalis deactivation**:
   - Wear kinesiology tape in X-pattern on chin overnight (zyotape)
5. **Zygomaticus major / Buccinator deactivation**:
   - Wear tape from top of ear along OG curve to cheeks while sleeping
6. **Neck training** (2-3x/week, repeat 2x):
   - Side neck curls with hand resistance (10 reps each side)
   - Front neck curls with weighted plate (10 reps)
   - Back neck extensions with weighted plate (10 reps)
- Test: swallow saliva and feel neck muscles engage; if not, neck is underdeveloped

## Claims to Verify
- "Your face is actually more adaptable than your body" — debatable; while facial bones do remodel, the body's larger muscles and bones also adapt significantly
- "Even things like xylitol will slightly disrupt your gut" — xylitol is generally well-tolerated in moderate amounts; can cause GI issues at high doses
- Implied guarantee that facial exercises will produce visible structural changes — evidence for facial exercises improving bone structure in adults is limited
