# Summary: How to Fix a Recessed Chin Naturally

- **Video**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vj5Gibm77v0
- **Channel**: Oscar Patel
- **Topic**: Natural methods to address a recessed chin through muscle activation and habit change

## Key Points
- A recessed chin is caused by bad habits (chewing, swallowing, breathing, posture), not genetics
- The suprahyoid bone position is controlled by a musculature sling (digastric, suprahyoids, masseters, genioglossus, hyoglossus) — not by bone itself
- Overactivation of the mentalis muscle creates chin dimpling and recession; deactivating it via kinesiology tape ("STTO method") can help
- Forward chewing (rolling lower teeth forward against back teeth) activates masseters and promotes mandibular development
- Tongue pressure on the incisive papilla (T-spot) is connected to chin projection via the anterior maxilla
- Acknowledges his own chin is technically recessed when measured, but appears normal due to optimizing other aspects

## Method/Routine
1. Train neck muscles (neck curls) to activate suprahyoids and reduce submental skin
2. Use AeroPit breathing device to activate digastric muscles
3. Suction mewing throughout the day: smile wide, open eyes wide, swallow to create suction
4. Wear kinesiology tape in X-pattern on mentalis muscle (chin) overnight to deactivate it
5. Rewire chewing: bite down and slowly roll lower teeth forward against back teeth to engage masseters
6. Tongue chewing: push gum against T-spot for 30 minutes daily
7. Combine with zygotape for additional tensile musculature sling effect
8. Fix overall posture, diet, and sun exposure
