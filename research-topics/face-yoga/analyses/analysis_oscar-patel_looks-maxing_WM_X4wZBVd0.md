# Summary: How to Actually Look Better in 2026 (Looks Maxing = Health Maxing)

- **Video**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WM_X4wZBVd0
- **Channel**: Oscar Patel
- **Topic**: Comprehensive guide to improving appearance through health optimization — covers breathing, posture, chewing, swallowing, mechanical techniques, diet, sunlight, and movement

## Key Points
- "Looks maxing is health maxing" — the central thesis; appearance is a byproduct of health, not genetics
- Four key myofunctional habits determine facial structure: chewing, swallowing, breathing, and posture
- Breathing should be "backwards" (horizontally into the nasal cavity, not upward into chest) at 4 sec in / 6 sec out
- Posture chain: feet -> legs -> lower back -> upper back -> neck -> head; misalignment cascades upward to the face
- Fascia is described as the body's largest sensory organ, dictating posture, movement, and facial structure
- Thumb pulling (3 min, 3-5x daily) on palate is the core mechanical technique; must be followed by mewing (tongue suction)
- References working with Dr. Mike Mew and the SMAS (superficial muscular aponeurotic system)
- Claims surgery always relapses because the tongue/habits didn't drive the change

## Method/Routine
1. **Breathing**: Breathe backwards (horizontally), 4 sec in / 6 sec out, silent breath
2. **Posture**:
   - Feet: slant board holds (60 sec, 2x), tennis ball foot rolling, flex anterior tibial tendon
   - Pelvis: RSS method (release fascia, stretch hip flexors, strengthen glutes)
   - Neck: weighted neck extensions and chin tucks off bench edge
3. **Chewing**: Forward bilateral chewing (roll jaw forward while biting), frown while chewing for masseter activation, 1 min intense chewing with mastic gum
4. **Swallowing**: Mona Lisa swallow (no facial muscle activation), tongue chewing for strength
5. **Mechanical techniques**:
   - Palate massage (back to front, 8.5/10 pressure, 1 min x 3)
   - Zygote pulling (thumbs under cheekbones, massage upward/outward)
   - Thumb pulling (3 min, stand against wall, chin tuck, push up and out on palate)
   - Hold breath before thumb pulling to decompress sutures
6. **Mewing**: Smile wide, say T, swallow repeatedly until tongue suctions; set hourly alarms as reminders
7. **Tape**: Zygomatic tape + mentalis tape while sleeping and during exercises
8. **Sleep**: Side position, fetal, pillow between legs, thumb pull 1 min before sleep, then suction mew, apply tape
9. **Diet**: Animal foods base (meat, seafood, raw dairy, fruit), biggest meal in morning, avoid overcooking
10. **Sunlight**: Morning/evening exposure to build solar callus, avoid chemical sunscreen, use tallow/coconut oil
11. **Movement**: Walk with toes in, barefoot sprinting, towel scrunches, mace bell exercises

## Claims to Verify
- "I expanded my pallet naturally more than people do who get pallet expanders" — no measurements or imaging provided
- "I grew my jaw forward more than people do who get double jaw surgery" — extraordinary claim without evidence
- "Nearly every surgery will relapse" — overgeneralization; many jaw surgeries are stable long-term
- "Levels of skin cancer are significantly higher in indoor workers than outdoor workers" — this is a real finding but context matters (window glass, type of skin cancer, etc.)
- "Eyesight improved from -10.5 to -3" — extremely unusual without surgical intervention
- Sunscreen critique based on seed oil theory — fringe hypothesis not supported by dermatological consensus
