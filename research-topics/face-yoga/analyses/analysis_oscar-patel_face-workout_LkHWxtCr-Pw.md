# Summary: Daily Face Workout to Change Your Health and Looks

- **Video**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LkHWxtCr-Pw
- **Channel**: Oscar Patel
- **Topic**: Step-by-step face exercise routine targeting 43 facial muscles for bone development and myofunctional health

## Key Points
- 43 muscles in the face can be trained to generate osteoblasts and develop new bone matrix
- The CPS (cheek puff and suck) exercise activates masseters, pterygoids, and other facial muscles; a clean popping sound indicates proper engagement
- Tongue tie stretch (tongue at T-spot/incisive papilla, mouth open wide, push backward) activates genioglossus and releases epicranial fascial tension
- Bilateral forward chewing with mastic gum is the "most important exercise" — engages mechanotransduction for bone development
- Tongue chewing (pushing gum against T-spot with tongue) engages genioglossus and hyoglossus muscles and supports hyoid bone
- Zygo tape creates anterior superior midface tension, encouraging tongue posture and disengaging mentalis muscle
- Aerofit breathing device on maximum setting activates masseters and digastric muscle while training respiratory muscles
- Suction mewing (tongue at T-spot, teeth together, swallow multiple times) is the foundational resting position

## Method/Routine
1. CPS exercise: puff cheeks with air, breathe through nose 10 sec, suck in cheeks, pop loudly with jaw forward — 7 reps
2. Tongue tie stretch: tongue at T-spot, open mouth wide, push tongue backward with finger — hold 20 sec, 2 reps
3. Bilateral forward chewing: split mastic gum in two, chew on both sides rolling jaw forward — 1+ min
4. Tongue chewing: one piece of gum, push against T-spot with tongue — 2-3 min intense
5. Apply zygo tape (cheeks + chin)
6. Aerofit device: maximum setting, bite down, breathe deeply — 2-3 min
7. Finish with suction mew: tongue at T-spot, teeth together, swallow multiple times

## Claims to Verify
- Claim that facial exercises generate osteoblasts and develop "new bone matrix" in adults — bone remodeling from muscle force is real but the degree of change in adult facial bones is debated
- "Within the first week you should notice facial muscles becoming tighter" — muscular adaptation plausible, but visible structural change in one week is unlikely
- Aerofit device marketed as beneficial despite being mouth breathing — the distinction made (exercise vs. habit) is reasonable but worth noting
