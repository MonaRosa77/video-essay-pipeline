# Summary: Natural Grooming & Skincare Routine

- **Video**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dt2rcxTpcTE
- **Channel**: Oscar Patel
- **Topic**: Chemical-free grooming routine for hair, skin, and teeth

## Key Points
- Rejects conventional skincare/haircare products as toxic and replaces them with natural alternatives
- Uses egg yolk (biotin) as shampoo to rebuild keratin in hair
- Uses unheated honey for hair texture and scalp health
- Brushes teeth with coconut oil on a boar hair bristle bamboo brush instead of conventional toothpaste
- Uses tallow as a moisturizer/aftershave (saturated fat as base for healthy skin)
- Applies emu oil on eyebrows for growth
- Emphasizes that diet and lifestyle are more foundational than any topical routine
- Morning sun exposure for circadian rhythm reset; drying hair in sunlight

## Method/Routine
1. Morning sun exposure to reset circadian rhythm
2. Wash hair with quarter egg yolk + unheated honey, brush with wooden comb
3. Use portable shower filter
4. Floss with silk floss
5. Brush teeth with coconut oil on bamboo/boar hair brush (baking soda 1-2x/week for whitening; optional hydroxyapatite toothpaste)
6. Use tongue scraper (stainless steel or copper)
7. Apply emu oil to eyebrows
8. Shave with Philips OneBlade
9. Apply tallow to face and neck
10. Apply coconut oil to body
11. Dry hair in the sun; do breathwork

## Claims to Verify
- "Toxic shampoo is cooking your hair" — mainstream dermatology does not classify standard shampoos as toxic
- "Biotin directly upregulates keratin" — biotin deficiency affects keratin, but topical egg yolk application efficacy is not well-studied
- "Saturated fat is the base of healthy teeth/skin" — oversimplification; dental and dermatological science does not support coconut oil as a toothpaste replacement
- Fluoride described as harmful — contradicts mainstream dental science consensus
