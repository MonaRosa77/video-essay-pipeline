## Claims to Verify
- "Blue light has literally been shown to make you gain fat" — some rodent studies show metabolic effects of blue light at night, but "toxin" framing is exaggerated
- "94% of people are completely deficient" in nutrients from organs — no citation for this specific percentage
- "4 in 5 people chronically over-breathe" — no source provided
- Transformation timelines (98 days, 8 weeks) for dramatic results — without controlled conditions these could reflect many variables (lighting, angle, weight loss, etc.)
- This video is primarily a sales funnel rather than educational content — less specific actionable detail compared to other videos
