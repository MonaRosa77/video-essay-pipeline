# Summary: How to Develop Your Bone Structure and Increase Bone Density

- **Video**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4rkYG6jeogI
- **Channel**: Oscar Patel
- **Topic**: Nutrition and lifestyle for building facial bone density

## Key Points
- Attractive facial features come from well-developed bone structure, not just skin/hair/fashion
- Three main "friction points" destroying bone density: fluoride, glyphosate, and polyunsaturated fatty acids (seed oils)
- Fluoride disrupts osteoblasts, competes with magnesium, lowers collagen synthesis, and harms thyroid/testosterone
- Glyphosate binds to calcium, magnesium, zinc, copper and prevents absorption; found in wheat, oats, corn, soy
- Essential nutrients for bone density: bioavailable calcium (1.5g+), collagen, vitamin K2, saturated fat, vitamin D3, magnesium, boron, zinc and copper
- Supplements are generally inferior to food sources due to poor bioavailability and harmful fillers
- Best food sources: raw A2 dairy, bone broth, hard cheeses, oysters, eggs, citrus fruits, dates, grass-fed meat

## Method/Routine
1. **Eliminate fluoride**: Stop tap water, switch to hydroxyapatite toothpaste or baking soda + coconut oil, replace non-stick cookware with cast iron/stainless steel
2. **Detox fluoride**: Increase saturated fat intake (raw animal fats); consume tamarind; increase boron and silica
3. **Eliminate glyphosate**: Cut wheat, oats, corn, soy; buy from local farmers; support liver with vitamin C, raw egg yolks, arugula, apple cider vinegar
4. **Eliminate seed oils**: No canola, soybean, corn, sunflower, safflower, etc.; cook with tallow, coconut oil, or A2 ghee
5. **Build bone**: Consume raw A2 dairy for calcium, bone broth for collagen, hard cheeses for K2, get midday sun for D3, eat dates/avocados for boron, oysters for zinc
6. Only supplement magnesium if needed; get everything else from food

## Claims to Verify
- Claim that fluoride "increases bone breakdown massively" (fluoride's relationship to bone is complex; low doses may increase bone density but make bones more brittle; high doses are harmful)
- Claim that all vitamin D supplements are "toxic" and "basically rat poison" from cholecalciferol (cholecalciferol is used in rat poison at very high doses, but therapeutic supplement doses are generally considered safe by mainstream medicine)
- Claim that bone structure can be meaningfully developed in adults through diet alone (bone density can improve, but facial bone shape changes in adults are very limited)
