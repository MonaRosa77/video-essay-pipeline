# Summary: How I Naturally Fixed My Crooked Teeth (No Braces, No Invisalign)

- **Video**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uXThK7lWFZ0
- **Channel**: Oscar Patel
- **Topic**: Natural correction of crooked teeth without orthodontic intervention

## Key Points
- Claims to have naturally straightened crooked teeth without braces or Invisalign by fixing underlying health habits
- Fascia (connective tissue) is foundational — constricted fascia prevents muscles from stretching and faces from developing properly
- The tongue acts as a natural retainer; proper tongue posture on the roof of the mouth is essential
- Thumb pulling combined with chin tucking is presented as the most effective mechanical technique (3 minutes/day, twice daily)
- Proper chewing (engaging masseters, 25+ chews per bite, harder foods) drives bone development through mechanotransduction
- Correct swallowing ("Mona Lisa swallow") using only the tongue, 650 times/day, generates significant force on teeth
- Breathing pattern of 4 seconds in, 6 seconds out loosens fascia and resets the nervous system

## Method/Routine
1. Loosen fascia via lymphatic drainage ("big six lymphatic routine")
2. Tongue stretching exercises: say "T" and push backward with finger; open mouth wide while holding tongue at T-spot
3. Thumb pulling on palate while standing against wall with chin tucked (3 min/day, 2x daily)
4. Full body warm-up (cobra pose, child's pose, neck stretching)
5. Rewire chewing habits: engage masseters, chew each bite 25+ times, eat harder foods (meat)
6. Fix swallowing: Mona Lisa swallow (no facial muscle activation, tongue only)
7. Breathing: 4 seconds in, 6 seconds out as base pattern
8. Sleep on side (not back), consume adequate calcium/A2 dairy, train back neck muscles
