## Claims to Verify
- "Fluoride directly calcifies your pineal gland and disrupts endocrine function" — pineal calcification is documented but the clinical significance and direct link to dark circles is not established
- "Walking barefoot on grass for 20 minutes lowers inflammation through electron transfer" — earthing/grounding research exists but is limited and controversial
- Raw carrot salad clearing "endotoxins" from the liver — this is a Ray Peat-influenced claim without strong clinical evidence
- Seed oils as a primary cause of dark circles — the causal mechanism linking dietary linoleic acid specifically to under-eye circles lacks direct evidence
