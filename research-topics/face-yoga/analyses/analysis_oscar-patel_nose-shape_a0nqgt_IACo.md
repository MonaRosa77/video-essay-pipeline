# Summary: How to Naturally Change Your Nose Shape (Without Surgery)

- **Video**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a0nqgt_IACo
- **Channel**: Oscar Patel
- **Topic**: Natural nose reshaping through tongue posture and facial exercises vs. rhinoplasty

## Key Points
- Nasal projection and shape are determined by the maxilla position, not solely genetics; tongue posture shapes the nasal floor and septum
- Rhinoplasty has high dissatisfaction: 44% of patients wanted revision; 37% developed breathing problems long-term
- Nose jobs relapse because they don't address underlying structural causes (tongue posture, jaw position)
- The maxilla developing upward and forward (from tongue on palate) straightens the nose and improves nasal projection
- Mouth breathing directly correlates with dorsal humps and downward nose growth
- Body posture is foundational: rib cage stacking, neck alignment, and foot position all cascade up to affect facial development
- Techniques shown: cervical fascia release with tennis ball, zygo tape + mentalis tape during sleep, zygo pulling, thumb pulling, suction mewing

## Method/Routine
1. **Fix neck posture**: Neck extension exercises to strengthen posterior neck; tennis ball under base of skull on floor to release cervical fascia
2. **Sleep protocol**: Apply zygo tape and mentalis tape nightly to encourage tongue-on-palate position during sleep
3. **Zygo pulling**: Put thumbs inside mouth by zygomaticomaxillary suture; massage upward and outward to release fascial adhesions
4. **Thumb pulling**: Perform 5-8 times throughout the day to catalyze palate expansion
5. **Suction mewing**: Maintain tongue on roof of mouth every minute of the day
6. Focus on the epicranial area (neck to top of head) as primary intervention zone

## Claims to Verify
- Claim that nasal shape is "determined not by genetics but through tongue posture and myofunctional habits" (genetics play a significant role; tongue posture influences development during growth years but cannot override genetic blueprint)
- Claim that 44% of rhinoplasty patients want revision (this statistic may be from a specific study population and not generalizable)
- Claim that adults can meaningfully shift their nose shape through tongue posture and thumb pulling (extremely limited evidence for bone remodeling in adults from these techniques)
- Claim that dorsal humps are caused by lack of pressure on the incisive papilla (oversimplified; dorsal humps have genetic, developmental, and structural components)
